Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ProspectLens analyze a website? ▼

When you open the side panel and click "Analyze", ProspectLens uses the standard Chrome Scripting API to read public HTML elements in the active tab. It inspects headings, telephone links, mailto addresses, social media links, structured Schema.org data, and CTA buttons completely client-side.

Does ProspectLens scrape LinkedIn or private social profiles? ▼

No. ProspectLens strictly analyzes the public website you are actively visiting. It does not scrape LinkedIn, bypass logins, access private member profiles, or harvest unlisted contacts.

How does GoHighLevel CRM integration work? ▼

You can paste your GoHighLevel Inbound Webhook URL into the ProspectLens Settings tab. When you click "Sync to CRM", ProspectLens sends an HTTP POST request with the company name, website, phone, email, health score, and tailored proposal angles directly into your GHL workflow.

Can I customize the client growth audit PDF? ▼

Yes! In the Settings panel, you can set your agency name, custom services offered, and consultation call booking URL. These details will be automatically branded onto every generated PDF audit report.

Where is my prospect data stored? ▼

100% of your saved prospects, pipeline notes, and audit history are stored in your local browser sandbox (chrome.storage.local). We do not operate remote databases storing your leads.
